Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

By using our website or providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

5. Tax Return Information

Information obtained in connection with the preparation of federal tax returns is subject to additional confidentiality protections under federal law.

We use and disclose tax return information only as permitted by applicable law, including Internal Revenue Code Sections 6713 and 7216 and applicable Treasury Regulations.

Where your consent is legally required for a particular use or disclosure of tax return information, we will obtain the required consent.

The use of third-party technology or service providers does not eliminate or reduce our obligations regarding the confidentiality and permitted use of tax return information.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We recognize that tax, accounting, payroll, and financial information is highly sensitive.

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal and client information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Access to client information is limited to individuals and service providers who require access for legitimate business or professional purposes.

We also use established third-party technology platforms, including Drake Software, Drake Portals (SecureFilePro), QuickBooks, ADP, and Google Workspace, as part of providing and supporting our professional services.

Although we take reasonable measures to protect information, no electronic transmission, computer system, cloud service, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Electronic Communications and Document Transmission

We provide clients with secure electronic methods for exchanging tax, accounting, and other sensitive documents.

Our primary secure client document portal is Drake Portals (SecureFilePro). Clients may use the portal to securely upload and receive tax documents and other confidential information.

We may also use Google Drive through Google Workspace for authorized electronic document storage and sharing.

Client information may additionally be processed or maintained within Drake Tax, QuickBooks Desktop, QuickBooks Online, ADP, and other systems used to provide professional services.

Because ordinary email may present security risks, we encourage clients not to send highly sensitive documents or information—such as Social Security numbers, passwords, complete financial account numbers, or copies of tax documents—through ordinary email when a secure document-sharing method is available.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, tax documents, financial account information, or other highly sensitive information through general website contact forms.
