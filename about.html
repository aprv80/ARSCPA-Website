About Us

Comprehensive Financial Services for Individuals and Businesses

We provide comprehensive accounting, tax, and advisory services to help individuals and businesses navigate complex financial decisions with clarity and confidence.

01

What we provide

Complete accounting, tax, payroll, and business formation services for individuals, entrepreneurs, and growing businesses.

  • Small-business accounting and bookkeeping
  • Bank and credit-card reconciliation
  • Income statements and balance sheets
  • General-ledger cleanup
  • Payroll services
  • Business and individual tax preparation
  • Tax planning and deduction strategies
  • Sales-tax filing
  • IRS representation
  • Company formation and incorporation
  • QuickBooks Online setup and guidance
  • Ongoing consultations and financial support
02

Why it matters

When your finances are accurate, organized, and understood, you can spend less time worrying about compliance and more time building a profitable business.

  • Business owners save time by not having to manage their books alone.
  • Accurate financial reports support better business decisions.
  • Organized records help businesses prepare for tax filings and audits.
  • Tax planning can help clients maximize deductions and avoid unnecessary liabilities.
  • Professional payroll and sales-tax support reduces administrative stress.
  • Flat-rate pricing creates cost transparency with no unexpected billing.
  • Personalized service gives clients a trusted financial partner.
  • Remote service makes professional support available beyond the local area.
  • Proper incorporation support helps international clients establish businesses legally in the United States.
03

How we work

We take the time to understand your situation, build the right service plan, and provide clear, timely support throughout the year.

  • We begin by understanding each client’s business, goals, and financial situation.
  • We tailor the chart of accounts and service plan to the client’s needs.
  • We offer monthly or quarterly support, depending on the business.
  • We use QuickBooks Online and/or QuickBooks Desktop to organize and simplify financial records.
  • We provide accurate, timely reports and responsive communication.
  • Clients can work with us remotely or through our Katy office.
  • We explain the numbers clearly and remain available for ongoing consultations.
  • We offer a free 15-minute consultation to discuss the client’s needs.
