Frequently asked questions

IT consultant accounting and tax questions

Do I report consulting income if I did not receive a Form 1099?

Generally, yes. Business income must be reported even when a client or payment platform does not issue an information return. Your own invoices, bank deposits, and payment records should support total receipts.

Do independent IT consultants pay quarterly estimated taxes?

They may need to. The answer depends on expected tax, withholding, credits, and prior payments. We can prepare projections and a payment plan based on current information.

Should an IT consultant elect S corporation treatment?

Not automatically. The possible tax effect must be weighed against payroll, reasonable compensation, filing costs, ownership eligibility, and ongoing administration.

How should payments to subcontractors be handled?

Collect appropriate tax information before payment, document the relationship, and review whether information reporting is required. Worker classification depends on the actual working relationship, not only the contract label.

Can you work with consultants serving clients across state lines or internationally?

Yes. Multi-state and international work can create additional filing, sourcing, foreign-reporting, or treaty questions that should be reviewed based on the consultant’s residency and activities.
