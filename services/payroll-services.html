Payroll Service

Reliable payroll processing and compliance support that keeps your team paid and your records accurate.

What we provide

We reduce the time spent on payroll-related administrative tasks through an efficient electronic payroll system. This system helps ensure timely employee payments, payroll tax payments, and accurate filing of payroll reports.

We also maintain a partnership with ADP to support a reliable and streamlined payroll experience.

Our payroll services include:

Payroll Processing

Efficient payroll processing for both paper checks and direct deposit, ensuring accuracy and timeliness.

Payroll Tax Management

Reliable handling of payroll tax payments to support compliance and reduce administrative burden.

Quarterly Reporting

Electronic filing of required quarterly payroll reports, including Form 941, TWC, and Form 940.

Year-End Reporting

Preparation and delivery of year-end payroll documentation, including W-2s and 1099s.

1099 Filing Support

Comprehensive 1099 e-filing services with distribution to recipients as needed.

Employee Access

Secure employee portals that provide convenient access to W-2s and recent pay stubs.
